Privacy Policy for Pickleball Rivals

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Pickleball Rivals (Android package com.ArtMindGames.Pickleball, the “Game”) collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have.

The Game is developed and published by ArtMindGames (“we”, “us”), which is the data controller for the processing described here.

Contact: artmindgames@gmail.com

1. Short summary

  • You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or photos.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device only.
  • The Game shows ads and uses analytics. To do that, our partners collect device identifiers and technical data.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads and analytics start, and you can change that choice at any time inside the Game.

2. What information is collected

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or precise (GPS) location.

4. Who receives the data

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with the service providers and advertising partners the Game integrates:

Advertising mediation and networks: Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies), Google AdMob, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral, Pangle, InMobi, Moloco, BidMachine, Bigo Ads, Ogury, Verve (HyBid), PubMatic, MobileFuse, Amazon Publisher Services.

Analytics and diagnostics: Google Firebase Analytics, Google Crashlytics, AppMetrica (Yandex).

Consent management: Google User Messaging Platform.

Store and payments: Google Play (Google LLC).

Other services: a public time service used to verify the current date for daily rewards, and a content delivery service used to download game content. These receive your IP address as part of the request.

These partners act as independent controllers or as our processors, depending on the service. Their own privacy policies are published on their websites:

  • Unity and LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google, AdMob, Firebase, Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Amazon Publisher Services: advertising.amazon.com/legal/privacy-notice

5. International transfers

Our partners operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws differ from those where you live. Where the GDPR applies, such transfers rely on the safeguards used by the relevant partner, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. How long data is kept

  • Data stored on your device remains until you delete the Game’s data or uninstall the Game.
  • Data held by our advertising and analytics partners is retained according to their own retention periods.
  • Data we receive for support requests is kept only as long as needed to answer you.

7. Your choices and rights

In the Game. Open the settings screen and use Privacy options to review or change your consent for advertising and analytics. This button is shown where consent rules apply.

On your device. Android lets you reset or delete your advertising identifier and opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting your data. Uninstalling the Game removes all data stored on your device. To ask us about data held by our partners, write to artmindgames@gmail.com.

If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you live in a US state with privacy rights, such as California, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Write to artmindgames@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

To handle any request, we may ask for information that helps us locate the relevant records, such as your device model and the approximate date you installed the Game.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at artmindgames@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add or remove a partner. The updated version is published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Significant changes will also be announced inside the Game.
